Play

Tap a card to send it to the best spot, or drag it. Tap the stock (top left) to draw. Build the four piles at the top from Ace to King.

Easy draws 1 card at a time. Hard draws 3. Winnable deal only deals games the solver has proven can be won.

Scoring: +10 for a card to the top piles, +5 from the stock to the table, +5 for turning a card over, −15 for taking a card back down, −2 every 10 seconds, and a time bonus when you win.
