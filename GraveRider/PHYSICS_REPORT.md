# Grave Rider — physics report

Generated by `node tests/physics-report.js`. Every number below comes from running the
game's own physics engine (Planck.js) with the bike set up as in `js/config.js`:
118 kg bike + 75 kg rider, 60 kW, 978 N·m at the rear wheel, 145 km/h top speed, 310/303 mm travel, grip 1.

## Quick physics check (pen-and-paper)

- Most acceleration the tyre can give on dirt: grip × g = **9.8 m/s²**.
- What the motor alone could push: 978 N·m ÷ 0.341 m ÷ 193 kg = **14.9 m/s²**.
- So below about 75 km/h the bike has more torque than the tyre or
  the wheelie limit can use. It is limited by grip and by the front wheel lifting, not by the motor.
- Steepest hill the tyre could hold even at a crawl (if all weight were on the rear): atan(grip) = **45°**. Real limit is lower because the front wheel lifts.

## Suspension

- Sag with the rider standing still: rear **98 mm** (32% of travel), front **66 mm** (21%). Typical motocross race sag is ~100 mm rear.

| Drop onto flat ground | Rear travel used | Front travel used | Rider crashes? |

| 0.5 m | 79% | 55% | no |

Over 100% means it hit the bottoming stop (a real bike would bottom out too).
The rider's legs are not modelled as springs, so landings here are a bit harsher than real life
(a real rider soaks up part of a landing with their legs).

## How much leaning actually does (in the air, 1 second)

- **Real life (flip assist 0):** leaning back for 1 s turns the bike **12° nose-up**; leaning forward **12° nose-down**.
  The rider and bike turn against each other (angular momentum is conserved), so a weight shift alone
  can't flip the bike. Real backflips come from the take-off ramp and the throttle/brake.
- **Game default (flip assist 1400 N·m):** leaning back for 1 s turns the bike **320°** (almost a full backflip).

## Throttle and brake in the air (0.5 s, leaving a jump at 40 km/h)

- Rear brake: nose drops **48°** (the spinning wheel's momentum moves into the bike).
- Throttle pinned: nose rises **83°** (real riders use this to lift the front).
- Throttle with rider control (game default): nose moves **0°** (the rider holds the throttle steady in the air).
